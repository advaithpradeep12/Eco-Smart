Waste Classification

AI waste recognition uses a browser-loaded vision model to estimate whether an image shows paper/cardboard, plastic, metal, glass, organic waste, or general waste. Keep this page open and the camera running for continuous monitoring. Predictions can be wrong and should be checked before disposing of waste. Research references: TrashNet (2,527 images) and TrashBusters combined (29,849 images). Open TrashBusters dataset · Open TrashNet · These are training datasets to download/train against; this browser prototype does not automatically ingest those datasets.
